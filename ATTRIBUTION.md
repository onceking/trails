# Attribution & Data Sources

This private repository archives and visualizes hiking routes from the **New York Ramblers Hiking Club** for personal planning and trail navigation. Full credit and attribution are given to the following organizations and volunteer contributors:

## 1. New York Ramblers Hiking Club
- **Official Schedule & Route Descriptions**: [New York Ramblers Schedule](https://www.nyramblers.org/schedule/)
- **Copyright**: © 2026 The New York Ramblers, Inc. All rights reserved.
- **Volunteer Hike Leaders (Fall 2026 Schedule)**: Hugues, Chris, Ingrid (substituting for Zee), Leah, Peter (Trails Committee Chair), Erik, and Brian.

## 2. Trail Maps & Cartographic Data
- **New York–New Jersey Trail Conference (NY-NJTC)**: Trail names, blazes, and map references ([Map 102 East Hudson](https://www.nynjtc.org/), [Map 118 & 119 Harriman–Bear Mountain](https://www.nynjtc.org/), and [Map 151 Norvin Green State Forest](https://www.nynjtc.org/)).
- **Long Island Greenbelt Trail Conference**: Trail network and bluff routes in [Sunken Meadow State Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sunken_Meadow_State_Park).
- **Westchester County Parks & NYS DEC**: Trail networks in [Salt Hill State Forest](https://dec.ny.gov/places/montrose-point-state-forest), [Edith G. Read Wildlife Sanctuary](https://parks.westchestercountyny.gov/read-wildlifesanctuary), and [Marshlands Conservancy](https://parks.westchestercountyny.gov/marshlands-conservancy).
- **Base Map Tiles**: © [OpenStreetMap](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright) contributors, SRTM, and © [OpenTopoMap](https://opentopomap.org/) (CC-BY-SA).

## 3. Public Transit Agencies
- **MTA Metro-North Railroad & Long Island Rail Road**: [MTA Schedules & Timetables](https://www.mta.info/schedules)
- **NJ Transit**: [Port Jervis Line](https://www.njtransit.com/abc_PORT_JERVIS) & [NJ Transit Bus 197](https://www.njtransit.com/)
- **Coach USA / ShortLine Hudson**: [Hudson Valley Bus Schedules](https://web.coachusa.com/coachusaassets/files/98/hudsonl.pdf)
